## Technical checks

Audio commands use the genuine output option `-ar` after the input and before the output filename. They do not use `asetrate` or retime unchanged values. Native and pinned WASM records support the four-second 24000 Hz worked WAV and 16000 Hz practice WAV, each with two audio channels and no video. The source's 48000 Hz is correctly described per channel, and stereo does not double this value. The page explains resampling as computing new values and explicitly separates metadata checks from listening; it does not promise identical timbre, unchanged audible detail or universal quality improvement. The accompanying native sine experiment supports preserved signal timing without pretending to measure Bunny pitch.

Independently ran the actual built practice cell in Chromium. The untouched 24000 Hz starter produced duration 4.000000, audio present and no video, but failed `Audio sample rate: expected 16000 Hz per channel; found 24000`. Changed the visible command to `ffmpeg -i bunny-short.mp4 -t 4 -vn -ar 16000 sceptic-samples.wav`, ran it, and checked again: duration, audio presence, no video and actual 16000 Hz all passed. This verifies sample-rate grading against real output rather than merely the command string. I did not make a subjective listening assessment in headless Chromium.

`audioSampleRateMatches` filters audio streams, requires audio to exist, and compares the numeric `sample_rate` on every audio stream. Its tests reject missing, empty, unparseable, nonfinite and wrong rates, a video-only field and mixed audio rates. Channels are not multiplied into the comparison. `Workspace.check` first verifies current command identity and input/output hashes, then uses `inspectMedia` on the actual blob. It also checks duration (truthfully labelled “close to” with a 0.16-second tolerance) and audio/video presence. Rate metadata is not used to claim matching sound.

The moved waveform remains the existing explicitly invented one-channel analytic 1 kHz sine over the half-open interval [0, 2 ms). The 4000/8000/16000 controls yield 8/16/32 samples, spacing 0.25/0.125/0.0625 ms, while the independent smooth curve and two cycles remain fixed. Independently changed the built selector to 16000 and confirmed its reported 32 points/0.0625 ms. The figure remains on the audio page after the definition, and its visible caveat says the low model rates are not suggested export settings. No claim ties density to louder, faster, higher-pitched or necessarily better audio.

It directs learners to actual destination guidance and short exports, including processed uploads.
